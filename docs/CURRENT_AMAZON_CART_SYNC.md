## Compatibility Notes From This Sync

- The current Amazon cart is below the $1,000 pre-tax target only because several required build/support items are not active in Amazon.
- Cameras, LTE modem, USB-C pigtails, and short USB cables are active, but the Raspberry Pi and microSD card are not. That is compatible only if a Pi and microSD are already available.
- The B6 charger is still active even though an OVONIC X1 Dual charger may be available. If the OVONIC charger will be used, remove the B6 charger from the Amazon cart and mark the charger as already-owned in `SHOPPING_LIST.md`.
- XT60 pigtails are still active even though MCIGICM XT60H connectors may be available. Keeping the pigtail kit is reasonable for quick wiring; delete it only if the team is comfortable soldering XT60H connectors directly.
