## Notes From Browser Verification

- Pololu drivetrain parts are the safest exact-fit purchases: #4742 motors, #1995 brackets, and #2686 adapters all showed live stock.
- Amazon pages showed the delivery location as Berkeley, CA 94704 and displayed delivery estimates on the items I opened. Most commodity items are linked as Amazon searches because the cheapest listing changes often; choose listings with Prime or delivery within about a week.
- For the wheels, buy the linked 120 mm / 12 mm-hex set or another set that is explicitly about 120 mm OD and 12 mm hex. Do not buy 88 mm buggy wheels; they will reduce clearance.
